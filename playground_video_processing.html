Video Processing Playground

Sends a video or audio file to POST /video-processing/process: rejects anything over 30 minutes, splits video down to 720p chunks (re-encoded one at a time) or audio-only input with stream copy when possible, transcribes every chunk with Qwen Omni, then restores one original timeline by shifting each chunk's [MM:SS] markers by its real start time. Video chunks are cut and transcribed with the next chunk's cut overlapping the previous chunk's Omni call, instead of waiting for every chunk to exist first.

Target length per chunk. Exact for video (each chunk is re-encoded, not keyframe-limited); audio boundaries can drift slightly since that path still uses stream copy.
30-minute input limit.
