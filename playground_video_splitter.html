Video Splitter Playground

Sends a video to POST /video-splitter/split, which splits it with ffmpeg's stream copy (-c copy, via imageio-ffmpeg) — no re-encoding, so it's fast and lossless, but cuts snap to the nearest preceding keyframe rather than the exact time requested. Each part is then transcribed with Qwen Omni OCR (omni_video.py, ported from ai-platform), and the ZIP includes a matching .txt transcript file alongside each video part.
